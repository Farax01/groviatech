PROOF

Work that speaks

Real fixes. Real results. Here's what happens when you prioritize clarity over complexity.

WEB3 TOKEN LAUNCH (PMV Meme, 2024)

From Confusing to Converting

A DeFi project had traffic but zero conversions. Their landing page used too much jargon and had seven different CTAs competing for attention.

The fix: Single clear value prop. One CTA. Wallet connection in 2 clicks instead of 6. Conversion rate went from 0.8% to 4.2%.

Lesson: Simplicity converts. Even in Web3.

SAAS STARTUP

Traffic ≠ Revenue

A SaaS company had 50K monthly visitors but abysmal signup rates. Their homepage looked like a feature list, not a solution.

The fix: Rewrote copy to focus on outcomes, not features. Added social proof. Reduced form fields from 9 to 3. Signups increased 217%.

Lesson: Show the result, not the process.

FOUNDER BRAND

Attention Without Direction

A founder had 30K Twitter followers but couldn't convert them to customers. Great content, zero funnel strategy.

The fix: Built a simple opt-in funnel. Created a lead magnet aligned with their offer. Added trackable links to content. First month: 400+ qualified leads.

Lesson: Attention is worthless without a path to conversion.
